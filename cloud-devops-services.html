Cloud & DevOps Services

GBA IT Solutions helps global businesses harness the power of the cloud and DevOps methodologies to accelerate innovation, improve agility, and optimize IT infrastructure. We provide end-to-end services for AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

Our Cloud & DevOps Service Offerings:

  • Cloud Consulting & Migration (AWS, Azure, GCP)

    Expert guidance to develop your cloud strategy, assess readiness, and execute seamless migrations to leading public clouds. We help you choose the right platform and services for your global needs.

  • Cloud-Native App Development

    Design and build scalable, resilient, and microservices-based applications optimized for the cloud. We leverage serverless, containers, and PaaS to deliver modern solutions.

  • CI/CD Pipeline Setup & Automation

    Implement robust Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) pipelines to automate your software development lifecycle, enabling faster releases and improved code quality across distributed teams.

  • Infrastructure as Code (IaC)

    Manage and provision your cloud infrastructure through code using tools like Terraform and CloudFormation. IaC enables consistency, repeatability, and scalability for your global infrastructure.

  • Containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)

    Utilize Docker for application containerization and Kubernetes for orchestration to enhance portability, scalability, and efficiency of your applications across diverse environments.

Transform Your Business with Cloud & DevOps:

  • Increased Agility: Respond quickly to market changes with flexible and scalable cloud solutions.
  • Faster Time-to-Market: Accelerate development and deployment cycles through automation.
  • Cost Optimization: Pay-as-you-go models and resource optimization in the cloud.
  • Enhanced Collaboration: Improve teamwork between development and operations globally.
  • Improved Scalability & Reliability: Easily scale resources up or down and build resilient systems.
  • Innovation Focus: Free your teams to focus on innovation rather than infrastructure management.
