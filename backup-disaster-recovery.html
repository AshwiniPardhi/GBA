Backup, Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity

GBA IT Solutions helps global organizations protect their critical data, ensure operational uptime, and maintain business continuity in the face of disruptions. We offer comprehensive backup, disaster recovery (DR), and business continuity planning (BCP) services tailored to your international footprint.

Our Business Resilience Offerings:

  • Cloud Backup Solutions

    Implement secure and automated cloud backup solutions for your critical data, applications, and systems across your global locations. Ensure your data is protected and recoverable from anywhere.

  • Disaster Recovery as a Service (DRaaS)

    Leverage our DRaaS offerings to replicate your IT infrastructure and workloads to a secure cloud environment, enabling rapid recovery in the event of a disaster at your primary global data centers.

  • Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

    Develop comprehensive business continuity plans that outline strategies and procedures to maintain essential business functions during and after a disruptive event, considering your entire global operation.

Ensure Resilience and Availability for Your Global Operations:

  • Peace of Mind: Confidence that your business can withstand unforeseen events.
